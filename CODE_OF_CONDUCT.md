# Community code of conduct

Alethia contributors and participants must communicate professionally and
respectfully.

Acceptable participation includes constructive technical disagreement, clear
feedback, respect for privacy, and taking responsibility for mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to `conduct@alethialabs.io`. The maintainer may
remove content, restrict participation, or ban accounts when necessary to protect
the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, subject
to legal and safety obligations.

This policy applies in repository spaces and in public interactions where a
person is representing Alethia or its community.
