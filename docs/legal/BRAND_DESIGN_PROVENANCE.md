# Brand and design provenance

## Founder declaration

Borislav Borisov states that he independently created the current Alethia
bracket-and-dot mark, wordmark composition, and the predecessor
the founder's pre-incorporation Alethia product design. This statement is a provenance record,
not a substitute for the signed founder IP assignment.

No employer, client, university, sponsor, collaborator, stock-logo provider, or
AI image generator is presently identified as a co-owner of those works. Any
contrary fact must be recorded here and escalated before release or trademark
filing.

## Design lineage

- The current Alethia system was developed from the founder-owned predecessor
  product and later normalized into reusable tokens and components.
- TOVR was used as an aesthetic reference. No TOVR source code, illustration,
  copy, custom animation, or proprietary asset is approved for inclusion.
- Generic monochrome palettes, spacing scales, technical typography, hairline
  borders, and conventional dashboard components are not treated as exclusive
  brand assets.
- Stale `Vertex` implementation names are migration residue and are being
  renamed to Alethia terminology.

## Logo evidence

Canonical vector sources live under `packages/assets/static/brand/`. Preserve
the earliest Git commits, editable vectors, exports, creation notes, and dated
specimens. Do not file the figurative mark until counsel completes an
image-similarity search. The closer public comparisons and the repository audit
are recorded in `docs/legal/DESIGN_SYSTEM_AUDIT.md`. The current device is on a
clearance hold; public visibility of similar bracket/dot marks means any
protection may be narrow.

## Release review

Before a material redesign or public campaign:

1. compare key screens against named references for literal similarity;
2. replace copied expression, not merely comments or identifiers;
3. update this ledger with new assets, authors, tools, and licences;
4. retain license texts and attribution required by fonts, icons, and libraries;
5. add commissioned-work assignments before accepting outside design work.
