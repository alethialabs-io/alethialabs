## AWS connection (second session)

The agent created a keyless provisioning role in the e2e account (556074788436) from the customer
module `connector-terraform/aws.tf`, with `issuer_url = https://dev.alethialabs.io/api/oidc` and
`role_name = AlethiaSandboxProvisionerRole`, and pasted only its ARN into **Connectors → AWS**:
"Connection verified". No access key or secret was entered anywhere. The role, its OIDC provider
and every resource the deploy left behind were deleted after the session.

## Not measured, and what unblocks it

- **Steps 2 (connect), 3–5, 9–12:** a cloud account connected to a sandbox org. The agent does not
  enter cloud credentials; the maintainer connects AWS (role) or Hetzner (token) and the session
  resumes from step 2.
- **Steps 10 and 18:** the private notes.
- **Step 16:** an identity provider (EDN-E2 brings one).
- **Step 19:** EDN-H1's throwaway-VM install.
- **Step 1, Pro half:** a deployment with Stripe test keys and the billing test-market switch the release gate sets.

## Defects filed

- #7289 — the AWS connector role lacks `iam:TagPolicy`/`UntagPolicy`/`ListPolicyTags`; every customer AWS deploy hangs. The e2e nightly uses its own role and never exercises the customer module.
- #7290 — cancelling a deploy mid-apply loses its state and strands its state lock; the DESTROY is held for 3 h and cannot clean up what the apply created.

- #7272 — canvas **Save** refuses a project with no cloud account, and the toast shows a raw field path.
