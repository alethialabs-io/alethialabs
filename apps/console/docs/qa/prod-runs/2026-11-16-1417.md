## 9 · Testability gaps

- **Unnamed controls.** The shell has two icon buttons with no accessible name. The new-project form has the region combobox and the four per-environment cluster-mode comboboxes. The add-component picker's options carry no name, and neither do two switches in New dimension. A pass cannot address these by role and name.
- **"Delete value"** in classification does not name the value. With more than one value the registry's
  `{ role: button, name: "Delete value" }` matches ambiguously.
- **`env.delete`'s `reach` is stale** (see section 5). The lane that replaced the menu with a row button
  did not update the registry.
- **The run needs a resizable window.** Before the next run, take Chrome out of full screen and set zoom to 100%.
- **An empty QA org would unlock more.** On Hobby, four journeys and nine registry entries are
  plan-gated. A Pro QA org without connected clouds would also allow `--allow-jobs`.
