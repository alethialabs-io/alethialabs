**Managed runners no longer hold long-lived cloud keys.** On AWS, GCP and Azure, a managed runner now
gets a short-lived token for the one job it is running, and the token is refreshed during a long
apply. There is no access key to rotate or to leak.

## New

- **More of the console in the CLI.** New command groups for drift and cost, promotions and
  delivery pipelines, environment protection and probes, and the project sources you attach
  (add-ons, Helm charts and your own IaC).
- **ArgoCD in the terminal.** `alethia cluster get` and `cluster list` show each cluster's GitOps
  sync and health.
- **More ways to install the CLI.** Scoop on Windows, and deb, rpm and apk packages on Linux, as well
  as Homebrew.
- **Project search on the server.** The projects page searches and filters on the server, and
  shows projects as cards or as a table.

## Improved

- The Evidence page groups receipts by project and links each check to its documentation.
- When a cluster does not become ready, the runner says why: which nodes are `NotReady`, or that the
  cluster rejected its credentials, instead of blaming the network.
- The Deploy tab shows the warnings from your deploy manifest.

## Fixed

- An apply that failed half-way could leave resources that blocked the environment for good. The
  runner now finds and repairs them.
- Lists that failed to load showed "nothing here yet". They now show an error and a way to retry.
- Opening a project that does not exist shows a "not found" page instead of an error.
