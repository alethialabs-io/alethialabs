**A kubeconfig in one click.** Every cluster card has a button that downloads a read-only kubeconfig,
valid for one hour, and shows the command for your cloud. In the terminal, `alethia cluster
kubeconfig` and `alethia cluster token` do the same, and `alethia logout` removes the cached
credentials.

## New

- **Export a project.** `alethia export` writes an existing project as `alethia.yaml`, so you can
  start managing it as a file.
- **Add-ons per environment** in `alethia.yaml`, through `init`, `plan` and `apply`.
- **Plans show a field diff**, and `apply` changes the fields you edited on existing components.
- **Look before you destroy.** Deleting something shows everything that goes with it, and you confirm
  by typing its name.

## Improved

- **Pages update by themselves.** Jobs, clusters, runners, alerts, environments, settings and support
  refresh when something changes, without reloading the page.
- Pages draw with their data on the first paint instead of showing a placeholder first.
- A job's page says what a drift scan or a plan found.
- `alethia plan` exits non-zero when the plan has problems, and `--detailed-exitcode` tells "no
  changes" from "changes".
- Paid features that are not on sale yet say so up front.

## Fixed

- On a self-hosted console served over plain HTTP, the copy buttons did nothing.
- Organization URL fields would not accept a hyphen.
- On touch devices, the delete control for a chat never appeared.
