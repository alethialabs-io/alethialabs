**Deletes that cannot catch you out.** You can no longer delete an environment or an organization
while an environment in it is live or still being changed. The console says what is in the way, and
the delete dialogs state the rule before you try.

## New

- **Re-runs ask first.** A destroy job cannot be re-run, and re-running a plan or a deploy asks you
  to confirm.
- **Runners pick only jobs they can run.** A runner skips a job that needs a capability it does not
  have, and leaves it for one that does, instead of failing it.

## Improved

- Cluster and environment status shows what the console last observed, not what it expected.
- When your session ends, one dialog explains why, and signing in again takes you back to where you
  were.
- Settings rows stack when the panel is narrow, and the mobile menu button names your organization.
- Shift+Tab moves focus backwards again; the console no longer uses it as a shortcut.

## Fixed

- A draining runner could flip back to online on its next heartbeat and pick up new work.

- Editing an alert policy could keep the draft of a different policy.
- Changing only a role's permissions was not saved as a change.
- Menus and popovers now match the width of the control that opens them.
