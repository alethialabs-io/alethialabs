---
title: Concepts
description: A map of how Alethia works — the model, how infrastructure is provisioned, how runners execute jobs, and how access and verification are secured.
---

Alethia provisions Kubernetes clusters and their backing services into your own cloud account, from
a design you make in the console or the CLI. A runner does the work: it plans with OpenTofu, checks
the plan, applies it, and installs ArgoCD so your apps deploy through Git.

  You["Console or CLI"] -->|design, plan, apply| CP["Alethia control plane"]
  CP -->|job| R["Runner"]
  R -->|short-lived credential| Cloud["Your cloud account"]
  R -->|tofu apply, then ArgoCD| Cluster["Your cluster"]
  R -->|logs, results| CP
