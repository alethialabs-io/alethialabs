A promotion copies the structural design of one environment onto another environment of the same or
a higher stage. Alethia plans the result, checks the target's protection gates, and then deploys it
or waits for a person to approve.

## How it works

### What is copied

A promotion changes what a resource is, not how much of it there is.

- **Structural** fields are copied: engine, version, topology, whether a component exists. For
  example, a database promotes its engine, engine version, port and IAM authentication setting.
- **Environment-tunable** fields stay as the target has them: sizes, node counts, retention, and
  environment-specific domains and repositories.

So promoting "add a Redis cache" from `staging` to `production` adds the cache and keeps
production's own sizes. A promotion with nothing structural to copy is refused.

### The steps

1. You promote from a source environment to a target. Alethia checks that you may `deploy` the
   project, that the target's stage is the same or higher (`development` → `staging` → `production`),
   and that the target is not queued, provisioning, destroying or destroyed.
2. Alethia records the promotion with a fingerprint of the source design, writes the merged design
   into the target, and queues a plan for the target.
3. When the plan succeeds, Alethia checks the gates below.
4. If every gate passes, Alethia queues a deploy that reuses the plan's frozen configuration. When it
   succeeds, the promotion is `SUCCEEDED`.

A failed plan or deploy marks the promotion `FAILED`. Only one promotion can be in progress for a
target environment at a time; a second one is refused before the target's design is touched.

### Protection gates

Each target environment has protection rules. With no rules, or every rule off, it promotes freely.

| Gate | Turned on by | Passes when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Predecessor healthy | Require predecessor | The predecessor environment is in sync (no drift) and has already deployed this exact design. |
| Verify | Require verify pass | The plan's [Elench](/concepts/elench) report has no failing hard control that is not waived. With no report yet, the gate waits. |
| Soak | Soak minutes | That many minutes have passed since the predecessor's last deploy. With no predecessor deploy, the gate fails. |
| Cost delta | Cost threshold (USD per month) | The plan's monthly cost minus the target's previous cost is not over the threshold. With no previous cost, the gate is skipped. |
| Manual approval | Require approval | Enough people have approved. |

The gates decide together: any **fail** blocks the promotion (`BLOCKED`); otherwise any gate that
waits moves it to `PENDING_APPROVAL`; otherwise it deploys. A cost over the threshold waits for
review; it does not block.

A [classification](/guides/classification) value on the target can force the approval or verify gate
on and raise the number of approvals needed. The strictest requirement wins.

### Approvals

When the approval gate waits, Alethia opens one approval slot per required approval.

- **Approve** fills one slot and checks every gate again. When all pass, the deploy is queued.
- **Reject** cancels the promotion.
- **Cancel** stops a promotion in progress. The design already written into the target stays.

The protection rules name who may approve: listed people, or a role. Owners and admins always satisfy
a role requirement. With no list, anyone who may `deploy` the project may approve.

## What it does not do

- It does not promote down: a target of a lower stage is refused.
- It does not copy sizes, counts or environment-specific domains.
- It does not undo the design it wrote into the target when you cancel.
- It does not add a job type. A promotion is a plan and a deploy.
