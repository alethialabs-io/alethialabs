Alethia is a control plane for Kubernetes on AWS, GCP, Azure and Hetzner. You design a cluster and
its services in the console or in one file, and Alethia provisions them into your own cloud account
with OpenTofu, checks every plan before it applies, and delivers your apps with ArgoCD.
