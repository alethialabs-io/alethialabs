---
title: Billing and plans
description: Fix a plan or trial that cannot start, a failed payment, and refusals from plan and usage limits.
---

Plans are managed on the **Billing** page in the console's settings. The plans and their prices are on
the [pricing page](https://alethialabs.io/pricing). The ceilings that apply to each plan are in
[Limits](/reference/limits).

Alethia is in Early Access. Paid plans open one market at a time, and Early Access has no uptime
SLA. The pricing page says which markets are open today. If it shows "Not on sale yet", no market is
open.

## "Alethia is not yet able to sell to customers in this country in this capacity."

**Cause:** paid plans are not open yet for your country, or for the way you buy: as an individual or
for an organization. Each country, and each way of buying, opens separately.

**Fix:** keep using the Hobby plan. It stays free, with no time limit. To hear when your market
opens, write to sales@alethialabs.io.

An organization in an EU country other than Bulgaria that has a VAT number can't be charged yet.
Alethia can give such an organization complimentary Early Access instead: the paid plan with nothing
charged and nothing owed. Billing starts only after the organization agrees to pay, and the free
period is never charged. To ask for it, write to sales@alethialabs.io.

## A purchase asks for more information first

The console shows one of these before it starts a purchase:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Accept the current Terms of Service before subscribing." | Accept the terms when the console asks, then try again. |
| "Tell us whether you are buying as an individual or on behalf of an organization …" | Choose one. The two carry different rights. |
| "Add a billing address before subscribing." | Add the address on the **Billing** page. |

## "Couldn't start the purchase. Billing may not be configured on this deployment"

**Cause:** the console could not start the payment. On a self-hosted console, billing is often not
set up at all.

**Fix:** try again. If it continues on alethialabs.io, open a support case from **Support** in the
sidebar.

## The Pro trial is not offered, or is refused

| Message | Cause |
|---|---|
| "This organization has already used its free Pro trial. …" | Each organization gets one trial. |
| "Your account has already used its free Pro trial. …" | Each person gets one trial, across every organization. |
| "Too many Pro trials have been started from your network recently, so a new one can't start now. …" | Your network reached the trial ceiling in [Limits](/reference/limits). |

**Fix:** you can still subscribe to Pro from the **Billing** page, when paid plans are open for you.

## The trial ended

**Cause:** the Pro trial lasts 30 days and needs no card. When it ends without a paid plan, the
organization goes back to the Hobby plan. Alethia sends an email when this happens.

**Fix:** nothing is deleted. Projects, environments, and clusters stay, and the Hobby plan's limits
apply again. To keep Pro, subscribe on the **Billing** page.

## "Payment failed"

The **Billing** page shows **Payment failed**: "The last payment for … didn't go through, so this
organization's paid features are paused until a payment succeeds." Alethia also sends an email.

**Cause:** the card was declined, expired, or needs your bank's confirmation.

**Fix:** select **Update the payment method**. If you see "Ask a member with the billing permission
to update the payment method.", ask a member who manages billing.

## "The payment did not complete — the card may have been declined, or your bank may need you to confirm it."

**Cause:** the payment for a new organization's plan did not complete. The organization is on the
Hobby plan until it is paid.

**Fix:** confirm the payment with your bank, or try another card. If the console says the payment is
still being confirmed, wait. A bank debit can take several business days.

## A feature says it needs another plan

**Cause:** the feature is part of a higher plan. The dialog says which one: "This feature is
available on the … plan."

**Fix:** upgrade on the **Billing** page, or compare plans on the
[pricing page](https://alethialabs.io/pricing).
