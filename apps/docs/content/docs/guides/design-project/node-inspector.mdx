---
title: Use the inspector
description: Rename a resource, change its settings, connect a service, read its status and cost, and duplicate, delete or destroy, from the panel beside the canvas.
---

Change a resource from the inspector, the panel docked on the right of the
[canvas](/guides/design-project/the-canvas). The board stays usable while it is open.

## Before you begin

- Open a project's **Architecture** view.

## Open and close the inspector

1. Select a card. With the keyboard, move to it with **Tab** and press **Enter**.
2. To close the inspector, select an empty area of the board, select **✕**, or press **Esc**.

Every subject has the same layout. From the top: **Back**, when you came from another subject. Then
the icon, kind, name and a summary. Then one suggested action, a status badge, the tabs, messages,
and the body.

| You select | The inspector shows |
| --- | --- |
| A resource | **Settings** and **Status**, and **Connections** for a service |
| The secrets vault | Its secrets, with a filter. A row opens that secret |
| An add-on | Its configuration and how it is installed |
| A bring-your-own chart | Its safety scan |
| A chart workload | What the chart describes, and its connections |
| A bring-your-own IaC card | The read-only list of its resources |
| The environment | **Settings**, **Sources**, **Status** and **Activity** |
| Several cards | **Duplicate** or **Remove**, as one undo step |

There is no **Save** button. Each edit goes into the design at once, and the changes bar counts it.

## Rename a resource

1. Select the name in the identity row.
2. Type the new name.

You can rename the project and any resource you can add more than one of. Names are lowercased as
you type, to match cloud naming rules.

## Change settings

1. Open the **Settings** tab.
2. Select a group to open it. A collapsed group shows a summary of its values.
3. Edit a field. The change applies when you leave the field, or when you close the inspector or
   select another card.

A group with a value that would stop the deploy opens by itself, and the problem shows under the
field. A group for one cloud carries a badge, such as `AWS only`. Groups for other clouds are not
shown. The inspector remembers which groups you open for each kind.

The cloud account is not set here. It is chosen once, when the project is created.

## Connect a service

1. Select a service and open **Connections**.
2. Add the database, cache, queue or secret it uses, and choose what it gets from each one.

A connection you draw on the board opens this tab too.

## Read status, endpoints and cost

The **Status** tab is read-only:

| Section | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **State** | The state and the reason for it |
| **Endpoints** | What the deploy produced, such as a database hostname, each with a copy button. Shown after a deploy. |
| **Drift** | Each OpenTofu address that drifted |
| **Cost** | The monthly cost from the last plan, per OpenTofu address. **Not priced yet** until a plan runs. |

## Duplicate or delete a resource

1. Open **Settings** and scroll to **Danger zone**.
2. Select **Duplicate** to copy the resource, or **Delete** to remove it. Press **⌘Z** to undo.

You can also select a card and press **Delete**, or right-click it and choose **Remove**. The
project, add-ons and bring-your-own charts have no **Danger zone**.

## Destroy an environment

1. Select the environment's name in the toolbar, or choose **Environment settings** from the **⋯**
   menu.
2. On **Settings**, select **Destroy**.
3. Confirm with **Destroy environment**.

This queues a job that removes the environment's cloud infrastructure. You cannot undo it.

## Check that it worked

The changes bar counts your edit as a pending change. After you deploy, **Status** shows the new
state and endpoints.
