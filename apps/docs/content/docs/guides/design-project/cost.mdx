title: See what an environment costs
description: Read an environment's monthly cost, each resource's cost, and what a plan changes, from the last Infracost estimate.
---

See the monthly cost of an environment and of each resource. Every figure comes from the last plan:
the runner prices each plan with Infracost. How the estimate is made is in
[Cost estimation](/concepts/cost-estimation).

## Before you begin

- The environment has been planned at least once. Until then, nothing is priced, and Alethia shows
  no figure rather than `$0.00`.

## Steps

1. Run a plan: select **Review plan** on the changes bar, then **Run plan**. The review view shows
   **Cost after this plan** as a monthly total.
2. For the environment's total from the last plan, open the **⋯** menu in the toolbar and read
   **Cost, from the last plan**.
3. For one resource, read the monthly cost on its card, or open it and read the **Cost** section of
   its **Status** tab. It lists the cost per OpenTofu address, most expensive first. A resource
   never priced says **Not priced yet**.

## What the figure leaves out

- It is as fresh as the last plan. After a design change, plan again.
- Infracost prices what is in the OpenTofu plan. Usage-based resources (NoSQL tables, queues,
  topics, bucket storage) and data transfer are not estimated.
- A plan whose cost breakdown is missing or broken shows as not priced, never as a wrong number.

## Check that it worked

After a plan, the **⋯** menu shows **Cost, from the last plan**, and each priced card shows its
monthly cost.
