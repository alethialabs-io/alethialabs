Open a job to follow its log as it runs, see what it found, and act on it.

## Before you begin

- Your role can view jobs. To cancel, deploy or re-run, your role needs the permission for that
  operation on the project. Without it, the button is disabled and says why.

## Steps

<Steps>

### Open the job
Select the job on the [Jobs](/guides/jobs) page, or follow a job link from the canvas.

### Follow the log
The log shows the runner's output as it arrives. Error output (`STDERR`) is styled as an error. The
log scrolls with new lines.

### Read the result
When the job ends, a banner under the header gives the outcome. For a successful plan or drift scan,
it says what the job found (see below). A failed job shows its error message.

### Act on the job
Use the buttons in the header:

| Button | Shown when | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cancel** | The job is `QUEUED`, `CLAIMED` or `PROCESSING` | Marks it cancelled. A job that has not started never runs. A running job's runner stops the run, but what it already applied stays. |
| **Deploy** | The job is a successful `PLAN` | Asks which runner to use (an online runner or **Any available**), then queues a `DEPLOY` of this plan. |
| **Re-run** | The job has ended, and its type can be re-run | A plan or deploy is queued again for the environment, with the project's **current** configuration, not the one this job used. An organization audit is copied as it was. |

A destroy is never re-run from a job: start it from the environment's settings. A runner job is
re-run from **Runners**. A cancelled job cannot be resumed.

</Steps>

## Result block

### Plan

The heading gives the number of changes, such as "4 changes planned", or "No changes — the
infrastructure matches the configuration." Under it, a count for each action:

```text
+1 to create · ~1 to update · −1 to destroy · ±1 to replace
```

If the job priced the plan, the block also shows the monthly cost change and the new total.

### Drift scan

The heading reads "N resources drifted" or "In sync — no managed resource has drifted." Under it,
the block can show:

- how many differences were dismissed as representational: a value written differently, not
  changed;
- whether unmanaged resources were checked. A refresh-only scan cannot see resources that are in
  the cloud but not in state, so the count leaves them out;
- the first five drifted resources, each with **Modified**, **Deleted** or **Other**, its address,
  and the attribute paths that differ. Values are never shown. Select **Show all N** for the rest.

See [Drift](/concepts/drift).

A job of another type shows "Job completed successfully." A stored result that cannot be read shows
"Plan summary unavailable" or "Drift summary unavailable"; the log still has the output.

## Details under the log

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Job details** | Job ID, type, runner, project, and when it was created, started and completed |
| **Config snapshot** | The project configuration when the job was created, as JSON |
| **Execution metadata** | What the runner reported, as JSON: OpenTofu outputs, cost, cluster details, and for plans and deploys the verify report in `verify_result` |

## Check that it worked

The status badge in the header shows the job's final status, and the result banner matches it.
