---
title: Give the assistant standing knowledge
description: Pin instructions and named knowledge documents to a workspace, so every chat in it starts with them.
---

Tell Elench once how to behave and what to know, so you do not repeat it in every chat. What you pin
goes into every chat in the current [workspace](/guides/assistant/workspaces).

## Before you begin

- The assistant is open in the workspace you want: the organization, or a project.

## Steps

<Steps>

### Open the knowledge panel
Expand the assistant and open **Knowledge** in the rail.

### Write instructions
Under **Instructions**, write how Elench should act here, for example:

> This is production. Always require approval before an apply, and never propose a destroy.

Instructions save when you leave the field.

### Add a document
Select **Add knowledge**, give it a title such as *Runbook: production deploys*, write the body, and
select **Save document**. It appears in the list with its size and when it was last edited.

### Edit or delete a document
Hover over a document to edit or delete it. A deleted document leaves every future chat at once.

</Steps>

Elench can tell you which document it drew on, because each one has its own title.

## Keep it short

Instructions and documents go with every message, so their size costs on every turn. A meter shows how
much of the 50,000-character limit you use. Write what Elench cannot work out for itself: conventions,
owners, on-call, runbooks and known problems.

## What Elench already knows

In a project workspace, the panel shows **Already known**: the live facts Elench reads on every turn.
These are the project's identity and region, its environments and their status, and its recent jobs.
Do not pin these. The organization workspace has no such section: its chats use what you pin and the
tools Elench calls.

## Check that it worked

Start a new chat in the workspace and ask Elench to restate your instructions. It quotes them, and
names any document it uses.
