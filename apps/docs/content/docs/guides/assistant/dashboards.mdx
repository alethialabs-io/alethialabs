title: Build a dashboard with the assistant
description: Turn Elench's answers into widgets on a grid beside the chat, or ask for a whole dashboard of your live infrastructure.
---

Collect Elench's answers as widgets on a grid beside the chat, and keep them up to date. Each chat
has its own grid.

## Before you begin

- The assistant is open. See [Ask the assistant](/guides/assistant).

## Steps

<Steps>

### Open the grid
Open it from the chat's top bar. It also opens when Elench builds something. The grid has five
columns and grows downward, in a pane beside the chat. Drag the divider to resize it. Drag it past a
quarter of the width and the pane closes; the chevron at the edge brings it back.

### Ask for a dashboard
Ask, for example, "Build a dashboard of my infrastructure: clusters, jobs, cost and drift." Elench
reads the data and lays out stat cards and charts.

### Fill one cell
Select an empty cell, type in **Describe what goes here…**, and press Enter. Elench reads one thing
and puts the result in that cell.

### Arrange the widgets
Drag a widget's grip to move it, or press Enter on the grip to move it with the keyboard. Drag its
corner to resize it.

</Steps>

## Widget controls

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Live** / **Frozen** | A live widget refreshes on its own. A frozen one keeps its snapshot. |
| **Refresh** | Reads the latest data now. |
| **Save** | Saves the widget, or the whole grid, as an [artifact](/guides/assistant/artifacts). |

## Check that it worked

The widgets show your organization's data, and **Refresh** changes the numbers when your
infrastructure changes.
