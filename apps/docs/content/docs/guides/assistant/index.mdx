Ask Elench, the assistant in the console, about your clusters, jobs, cost and drift. It answers from
your real state. When you ask it to change something, it proposes the change; the verification
engine and a person still decide.

## Before you begin

- On the hosted service, the assistant is on. A self-hosted console needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`. Without
  it, **Ask AI** does nothing and the assistant reports that it is not configured.
- Your organization's AI plan has allowance left. The **Usage** card on the overview shows the
  current session and the week.

## Steps

<Steps>

### Open the assistant
Select **Ask AI** at the top right of any page. It opens as a panel beside your work. **Expand** makes
it full screen, with a list of chats, the knowledge panel and the dashboard grid. **Minimize** docks it
again. The chat carries on in both.

### Pick the workspace
Open it from an organization page for an organization chat, or from a project's pages or canvas
for a chat about that project. See [Workspaces](/guides/assistant/workspaces).

### Tag what you mean
Type `@` and pick a **project**, **cluster**, **job**, **connector**, **runner**, **cloud identity** or
**artifact**. The tag becomes a pill, so Elench knows exactly which resource you mean.

### Choose how much it may change
The pill on the composer sets the editing mode:

| Mode | What Elench does with a change |
| --- | --- |
| **Ask** (default) | Drafts the change and waits for you to approve each one. |
| **Auto** | Applies edits in this chat without asking each time. |

Neither mode skips the deploy gate: a real deploy still runs the verify checks and needs a person.

### Choose the response style
In the composer's settings, **Fast** suits everyday questions and **Thinking** suits complex work.
On the **Max** tier, **Deep reasoning** spends more on one hard question.

</Steps>

## What it can and cannot do

| It can | It cannot |
| --- | --- |
| Read what your role can read in this organization, through the same permission checks as the console | Read another organization's data |
| Answer with widgets on a [dashboard](/guides/assistant/dashboards) | Invent state it cannot read |
| Propose a plan or a deploy that a person accepts | Apply a change on its own: the [verification engine](/concepts/elench) runs between `tofu plan` and `tofu apply`, and a person approves the deploy |

Usage counts against your organization's AI plan.

## Check that it worked

Ask "Which of my jobs failed this week?". The answer names real jobs from your organization.
