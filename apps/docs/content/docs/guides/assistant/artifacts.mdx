---
title: Save and share a dashboard
description: Save an assistant dashboard as a named artifact, open it in any chat, and share it with your organization, a team or a role.
---

Keep a [dashboard](/guides/assistant/dashboards) after its chat ends, and share it. A saved dashboard
is an **artifact**. Its widgets stay tied to the tools that made them, so they refresh against live
data when you open it in a chat.

## Before you begin

- You have a dashboard on a chat's grid.
- To share, your organization is on a paid plan and has more than one member.

## Steps

<Steps>

### Save it
On the grid, or on one widget, select **Save as artifact** and give it a name. The name must be unique
in your library. A new artifact is private to you.

### Find it
Open **Artifacts** in the rail. **Yours** lists what you saved. **Shared with you** lists what others
shared with your organization, a team you are on, or a role you hold. Each card shows the kind and the
number of widgets.

### Open it
Select an artifact. It opens read-only, as it was saved, and changes no chat.

### Act on it
On an artifact you own:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Add to this chat** | Puts its widgets on the open chat's grid. |
| **Open in new chat** | Starts a chat with the artifact on its grid. |
| **Share** | Shares it with the whole organization, a team or a role. |
| **Delete** | Removes it from the library. |

An artifact someone shared with you is view-only.

</Steps>

To start one from nothing, select **New artifact**: a new chat opens with a few prompts to begin.
In any chat, tag an artifact with `@` to ask Elench to explain or extend it.

## Check that it worked

The artifact is under **Yours**. A teammate you shared it with finds it under **Shared with you**.
