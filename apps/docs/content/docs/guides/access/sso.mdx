---
title: Set up single sign-on
description: Let members sign in through your own identity provider over OIDC or SAML, by registering the provider and verifying your email domain.
---

Let your members sign in through your identity provider (IdP), such as Okta, Microsoft Entra ID,
Google Workspace, AWS IAM Identity Center, OneLogin or Ping. Alethia is the service provider: it uses
your IdP and runs none of its own. Each organization registers its own providers, so organizations
can use different IdPs.

## Before you begin

- The organization is on the Enterprise plan. On other plans, the page offers an upgrade.
- Your role can manage SSO.
- You can create an OIDC or SAML application in your IdP, and add a DNS record for your email domain.
- A self-hosted console has the enterprise setup done. See [Enterprise setup](/self-host/enterprise-setup).

## Steps

<Steps>

### Start the registration
Open **Settings → Single Sign-On** and select **Register provider**.

### Choose the protocol
Pick **OpenID Connect** or SAML. Protocol details are in [OIDC](/reference/standards/oidc) and
[SAML and SCIM](/reference/standards/scim-saml).

### Enter the IdP's details

| OIDC | SAML |
| --- | --- |
| **Issuer URL** | **IdP Entity ID (issuer)** |
| **Client ID** and **Client secret** from the IdP's OIDC application | **IdP sign-on URL** and **Signing certificate (X.509)** from the IdP's SAML metadata |

### Set the domain and mapping
Enter a **Provider id**, a short slug such as `okta`, and the **Email domain** whose users sign in
through this provider, such as `company.com`. Change **Email claim** and **Name claim** if your IdP
uses other names.

### Review and register
Check the summary and register the provider. It shows **Pending domain**.

### Configure your IdP
The provider shows the **Redirect URI** (OIDC) and the **Start URL**. Add them to your IdP application.

### Verify the domain
Select **Show DNS record**, add that record to your domain's DNS, then select **Verify domain**.
When it passes, the provider shows **Verified**, and SSO sign-in is on.

</Steps>

## Check that it worked

Select **Test connection** on the provider. Then sign in at the **Start URL** with an account on the
email domain.

The first time someone signs in through SSO, Alethia adds them to the organization with the
least-privileged role. From then on they are an ordinary
[member](/guides/access/members-and-invitations), and you change their access with
[grants](/guides/access/access-portal). SCIM provisioning and deprovisioning from your IdP is not
available yet: users are added when they first sign in.

## Edit or remove a provider

**Edit provider** changes the details. Leave the client secret or certificate blank to keep the
stored one. **Remove provider** stops sign-in through it; the members' accounts and memberships stay.
It cannot be undone.
