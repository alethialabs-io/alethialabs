---
title: Create teams
description: Group members into a team and grant access to the team once, so access follows team membership.
---

Grant access to a group of members at once. Give a team one grant, and everyone on it has that
access. Add or remove people, and their access follows. Sharing a project with five engineers is then
one grant, not five.

## Before you begin

- The organization is on the Enterprise plan.
- Your role can manage teams. Without it, the page says so.

## Steps

<Steps>

### Create the team
Open **Settings → Teams**, select **Create team**, and name it, for example `platform`.

### Add members
Select **Manage members** on the team and add or remove organization members.

### Grant access to the team
In **Settings → Access**, add a grant and pick the team as the principal. See
[Grant access](/guides/access/access-portal).

</Steps>

A team grant applies to a user for as long as they are on the team. Removing someone from the team
removes that access at once. You can grant a team a [custom role](/guides/access/custom-roles), scope
the grant to one project, and still deny one member something on top.

## Check that it worked

The team lists its members, and **Settings → Access** shows the grant with the team as principal. A
member of the team can do what the grant allows.
