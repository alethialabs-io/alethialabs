Create a shared organization so others can work on your projects, and switch between the
organizations you belong to. Every project, runner, cloud identity and job belongs to one
organization, and its members see only that organization's resources.

## Before you begin

- You have your **personal** organization already. It is made with your account, it is on the free
  plan, and you are its only member and its `owner`.
- To share with others, you need an organization on a paid plan. Inviting members comes with Pro and
  Enterprise.

## Create an organization

1. Open the organization switcher at the top of the sidebar.
2. Select **Create organization**.
3. Name it, choose a plan and pay, then invite members.

You become the new organization's `owner`. See
[Invite members](/guides/access/members-and-invitations).

## Switch organizations

Open the switcher and pick the organization. It lists each organization you belong to with its plan,
and marks the active one. Your personal organization shows as **Personal**.

The active organization decides what you see and what you may do. Every permission check, list and
action runs in it. From the CLI, run `alethia org switch`.

## How roles work in an organization

Each member has a role in the organization:

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| `owner` | Every permission, including billing |
| `admin` | Every permission except billing |
| `operator` | Plan, deploy and destroy projects and runners. Cannot manage cloud identities, members or billing. |
| `viewer` | Look only |

A role can apply to the whole organization or to one project. The full list of permissions is in
[Roles and permissions](/concepts/access-control/roles-and-permissions).

The database enforces the boundary too. PostgreSQL row-level security returns only the active
organization's rows, so a flaw in the application still cannot show another organization's data. See
[Security](/concepts/security).

## Check that it worked

The switcher shows the new organization as active, and **Overview** lists only its projects.
