---
title: Create a custom role
description: Build a role with exactly the permissions you choose, from a matrix grouped by resource.
---

Make a role with exactly the permissions you need, such as "can plan and deploy projects, but never
destroy". A custom role lives in your organization next to the four built-in roles, and you grant it
the same way.

## Before you begin

- The organization is on the Enterprise plan.
- Your role can manage roles.

## Steps

<Steps>

### Open the editor
Open **Settings → Roles** and select **New role**.

### Pick the permissions
The matrix groups permissions by resource:

| Group | Example permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Projects | `project:view`, `project:create`, `project:edit`, `project:plan`, `project:deploy`, `project:destroy` |
| Runners | `runner:view`, `runner:create`, `runner:edit`, `runner:deploy`, `runner:destroy` |
| Cloud identities | `cloud_identity:view`, `cloud_identity:manage_identities`, `cloud_identity:test` |
| Others | Jobs, connectors, organization, members, alerts, fleet, support, activity, billing |

Each kind of job is its own permission. A role can hold `project:plan` without `project:deploy`, or
`runner:deploy` without `runner:destroy`.

### Save the role
Name the role and save it.

</Steps>

To grant it, see [Grant access](/guides/access/access-portal), or pick it when you
[invite a member](/guides/access/members-and-invitations).

When you change a role's permissions later, every grant that uses it changes at once. You do not
re-assign anything. For a one-off, grant a single permission instead of making a role.

## Check that it worked

The role is listed in **Settings → Roles**, and appears as a choice when you add a grant.
