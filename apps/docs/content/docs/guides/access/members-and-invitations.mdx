---
title: Invite members
description: Invite people to an organization by email with a role, change a member's role, and remove members.
---

Bring people into your organization with the role they need. A member's role turns into their
access at once: there is no separate grant to manage.

## Before you begin

- The organization is on the Pro or Enterprise plan. A personal organization has one member: you.
- Your role can manage members (`owner` or `admin`).

## Steps

<Steps>

### Open the invite dialog
Open **Settings → Members** and select **Invite members**.

### Enter emails and roles
Enter each email and pick a role: one of the four built-in roles, or a
[custom role](/guides/access/custom-roles). The dialog refuses an address that is already a member,
already invited, or entered twice.

### Send
Alethia sends each person an email with an accept link.

</Steps>

The invitee opens the link. If they are not signed in, they sign in first and come back. Email sign-in
also creates an account, so they do not need one beforehand. Accepting makes them a member with the
role you chose.

## Change or remove members

- Change a member's role in the members table. Their access changes at once.
- Select **Remove member** on one row, or select several and **Remove selected**. Their access ends
  at once.

For finer access, such as one project or an explicit deny, add a grant after they join. See
[Grant access](/guides/access/access-portal).

## Check that it worked

The new member appears in **Settings → Members** with their role. They see the organization in their
switcher.
