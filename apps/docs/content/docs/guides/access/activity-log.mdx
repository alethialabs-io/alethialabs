---
title: Read the activity log
description: See every action and every refused attempt in your organization or a project, filter it, and export it as CSV on Enterprise.
---

Find out who did what, and who tried something they were not allowed to do. The activity log records
every change in the organization.

## Before you begin

- Your role can view activity. Every built-in role can.
- How far back you can read depends on the plan: 7 days on Hobby, 30 on Pro, 365 on Enterprise.
- Export needs the Enterprise plan.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Activity** for the organization, or a project's **Settings → Activity** for one
   project.
2. Pick a time range: **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, or a **Custom range**.
3. Narrow it with the search (**Search actor, action or resource…**), **Member**, **Project** and
   **Events**.
4. Select **Load more** to read further back.

Each entry is one sentence: who did what, to which resource, and when. Refused attempts are marked.
A person who left the organization shows as **Former member**.

## What is recorded

| Recorded | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Every action | Create, edit, deploy, destroy, managing identities, members, connectors and billing, exports |
| Every refused attempt | Someone tried an action their role does not allow |
| Access changes | Custom roles created, changed or deleted; grants added or revoked; members joining, leaving or changing role |

Successful reads, such as opening a page or a list, are not recorded: there are too many. A refused
read is recorded.

## Export the log

Select **Export CSV**, for a SIEM, a spreadsheet or an archive. On other plans, the button says that
export needs Enterprise, and the server refuses an export too.

## Check that it worked

Make a small change, such as renaming a project. Its entry appears at the top of the log, with your
name.
