---
title: Grant access
description: Add a grant in Settings → Access, choosing who, allow or deny, a role or permission, and a scope, and revoke grants you no longer need.
---

Give a member or team access to the organization or one resource, or take it away. **Settings →
Access** lists every grant in the organization. A project's **Settings → Access** lists the grants on
that project.

## Before you begin

- The organization is on the Enterprise plan.
- Your role can manage access.
- You know the parts of a grant. See [How grants and scopes work](/guides/access/grants-and-scopes).

## Steps

<Steps>

### Start a grant
Open **Settings → Access** and select **Add grant**.

### Pick who
Pick a **Member or team**. A team grant reaches everyone on the team.

### Allow or deny
Leave it as an allow, or choose **Deny** for an exception that overrides any inherited allow.

### Pick what
Choose a **Role**, built-in or [custom](/guides/access/custom-roles), or a single permission such as
`project:deploy`.

### Pick the scope
Choose **Entire organization**, or one project, runner or cloud identity. A grant on the organization
flows down to everything in it.

### Save
The grant takes effect at once.

</Steps>

To take access away, select **Revoke access** on the grant's row.

## Find a grant

Search by member, team or scope, and filter by scope, role and effect. Each row shows the principal,
the effect, the role or permission, the scope, its **Effective reach**, and when it was granted.

## Check that it worked

The grant appears in the list. Sign in as the member, or ask them, to confirm that they can do what
the grant allows and nothing it denies.
