In Claude on claude.ai and in the Claude desktop app, Alethia is a **custom connector**. Through it,
Claude can read your projects, jobs and clusters, change designs, start a plan, and ask you to
approve an apply or a destroy in the console. It cannot approve anything itself.

## Before you begin

- Your console origin: the address you sign in to Alethia at. The MCP endpoint is that origin plus
  `/api/mcp`.
- A Claude plan that lets you add custom connectors. On a team plan, an owner may need to add the
  connector for the organization first.

## Add the connector

<Steps>

### Open the connector settings

In Claude, open **Settings**, then **Connectors**, and choose **Add custom connector**.

### Enter the endpoint

Give the connector a name, for example `Alethia`, and enter your endpoint:

```text
https://<your-console-origin>/api/mcp
```

### Sign in

Choose **Connect**. Your browser opens the Alethia sign-in page. Sign in, and approve access if the
page asks.

### Use it in a chat

Turn the connector on for a chat and ask about your infrastructure, for example "which of my
Alethia jobs failed this week?".

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  The server speaks only the current generation of the MCP protocol and expects a Client ID Metadata
  Document from the client. If the connection fails, see
  [Connect a client](/guides/assistant/mcp#connect-a-client).
</Callout>

The connector acts as you, in one organization: see
[which organization the tools read](/guides/assistant/mcp#which-organization-the-tools-read).
