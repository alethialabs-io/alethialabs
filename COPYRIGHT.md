# Copyright and chain of title

## Current status

ALETHIA LABS EDPK was registered in Bulgaria on 11 August 2026 with EIK 208913663. The founder currently owns the founder-created pre-incorporation
software, documentation, logo, design system, domains, and related works, subject
to the rights of third-party dependencies and contributors.

The post-registration founder assignment is prepared but is not effective until
the sole-owner approval, controlling Bulgarian agreement, and asset schedules are
signed. Repository ownership notices must not be switched before that execution.

## Contributors

External contributors retain ownership of their contributions. Contributions are
accepted under the repository licence. Once the post-registration CLA is active,
contributors also grant the registered company the broad, non-exclusive rights
stated in that CLA, including the right to offer accepted contributions under
commercial terms.

A CLA is not an assignment. Alethia will not describe contributor copyright as
company-owned unless a separate written assignment exists.

## Required activation steps

1. Sign the sole-owner approval, founder agreement, and attached schedules.
2. Record the deferred consideration and acquired right in the company records.
3. Activate a versioned ICLA and CCLA and record the exact document hash.
4. Update this notice and the current licensor record without rewriting file
   histories or removing contributor attribution.
